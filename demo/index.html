How it works, in three steps

No changes to how your agents are built. You point their app connections through Sentinel, and from then on every action passes a check first.

1

Connect

Your agents reach SAP, Stripe, Gmail, Zendesk and other apps through Sentinel instead of directly — like employees badging through a security gate.

2

Check

Each action is compared with your rules, scanned for hidden instructions and checked against real data. It is allowed, stopped, or sent to a person.

3

Control

You see everything live, approve or reject risky actions, read how each decision was made, and can pause or stop any agent in one place.

Questions

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. The console starts in Simple mode with plain words, a guided tour and explanations on every term. Expert mode adds the technical details for IT teams.

Is the demo connected to anything real?

No. The demo company "Acme Italia" and all its agents, payments and events are simulated in your browser. Nothing leaves your computer.

What happens if nobody answers an approval?

It is rejected automatically after 15 minutes. Sentinel never lets a risky action through by default.

Which AI models and apps does it work with?

Sentinel checks actions, not models, so it works with agents built on any model. The demo shows connections to SAP, Stripe, Gmail, HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack and others.
